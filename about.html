Our story

AuntyHR is a brand built on passion for grooming talents and helping them reach their maximum potential. We also know that great talents need great spaces to thrive, which makes people operations an integral part of our work.

So we prioritize creating the systems and processes that bring balance between employers and employees — the kind of balance that drives both career and business growth. Businesses, professionals, systems, processes: that’s the work, and it’s been the work since 2023.

Meet the founder

Mfonobong Eshett

The Aunty behind AuntyHR

Mfonobong Eshett, PHRi™ · HR Manager, Crop2Cash · Founder, AuntyHR

Mfonobong Eshett is a certified Human Resource Professional specializing in coaching, learning and development for individuals and organizations. With nearly a decade of experience, she has designed and led employee programs that enhance performance and foster organizational growth — work that has reached 3,000+ professionals and 50+ businesses to date. She currently serves as Human Resources Manager at Crop2Cash, alongside founding and leading AuntyHR.

She is the author of Make Them Choose You, a practical guide to career growth and employability, and is passionate about building people-first organizations — equipping professionals with the skills to thrive in today’s evolving workplace. Her expertise spans strategic HR analytics, employability skills, leadership development and workforce transformation.

Driven by a passion for career development, especially for young professionals, she builds structures that maximize workforce potential across Fintech, Agritech, Edtech, Medtech, Nonprofits, Media, Hospitality, Health, Catering, Beauty & Cosmetics and FMCG.
